Privacy Policy — Quest365

Last updated: 30/12/2025

Quest365 respects your privacy. This Privacy Policy explains how information is handled when you use the Quest365 mobile application.

1. Information We Collect

Quest365 is designed to be used without creating an account.

Depending on how you use the app, the following data may be processed:

  • App usage data
    Completed challenges, progress, XP, and in-app preferences. This data is primarily stored locally on your device.
  • Anonymous analytics & diagnostics
    Aggregated data such as app interactions, crashes, or performance metrics used to improve stability and user experience.
  • Device information
    Basic technical information such as device type and operating system version.

Quest365 does not collect:

  • Location data
  • Contacts
  • Photos or media
  • Financial or payment information

2. How We Use Information

Any data processed is used only to:

  • Provide core app functionality (daily challenges, progress tracking)
  • Improve app performance, reliability, and design
  • Fix bugs and crashes
  • Understand anonymous usage patterns

Quest365 does not use data for advertising personalization.

3. Data Storage & Security

  • Most user progress data is stored locally on your device.
  • Anonymous analytics and crash data (if enabled) are handled using industry-standard security practices.
  • Data is encrypted in transit where applicable.

4. Data Sharing

Quest365 does not sell personal data.

Limited anonymous data may be processed by trusted third-party services strictly for app operation, such as:

  • Analytics services (e.g. Firebase)
  • Crash reporting tools

These providers are required to handle data securely and only for providing their services.

5. Data Deletion Requests

You may request deletion of any data associated with Quest365.

What data can be deleted:

  • Anonymous analytics or diagnostic data associated with your device, where applicable.

What data is not stored remotely:

  • Challenge progress and preferences stored locally on your device can be removed at any time by uninstalling the app.

How to request deletion:

Email kejolcompany@gmail.com

Please include “Quest365 Data Deletion Request” in the subject line.

6. Changes to This Policy

This Privacy Policy may be updated from time to time. Any changes will be posted on this page with an updated revision date.
