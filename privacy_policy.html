Privacy Policy

App: justinTime
Effective date: 01/03/2026
Contact: kejolcompany@gmail.com

justinTime is a deadline reminder app designed to help you avoid missing return/refund/free-trial windows. This Privacy Policy explains what information we collect, how we use it, and the choices you have.

1) What we collect

A) Information you enter

  • Deadlines you create (e.g., title, type, due date, optional amount, notes).
  • Receipts you choose to attach (files/images you select).

B) Device permissions (optional)

  • Notifications: used to send you reminders you schedule.
  • Files/Photos/Camera (if you use receipts): used only to let you pick or capture receipt images/files.

C) Purchases

  • If you purchase premium features, the transaction is processed by Google Play. We do not receive your full payment details (such as card numbers).
  • We may receive a purchase state/entitlement status from Google Play to unlock features in the app.

2) What we do not collect

  • We do not collect your name, phone number, or contact list.
  • We do not collect precise location data.
  • We do not sell your personal data.

3) How we use your information

  • To store and display your deadlines and receipts inside the app.
  • To schedule and deliver reminder notifications you request.
  • To restore or confirm premium access (if you purchase).
  • To troubleshoot app issues (for example, crash logs if enabled by your device/OS).

4) Where your data is stored

By default, your deadlines and receipts are stored locally on your device. If we add cloud sync in the future, we will update this policy before enabling it.

5) Sharing of information

We do not share your personal information with third parties for advertising. The app may interact with:

  • Google Play Billing (to process purchases and restorations).
  • Android system services (to schedule alarms/notifications).

6) Data retention

Your deadlines and receipts remain on your device until you delete them or uninstall the app.

7) Your choices and controls

  • You can edit or delete deadlines at any time.
  • You can remove receipts attached to a deadline.
  • You can disable notifications in your device settings (the app will continue to work, but reminders won’t appear).

8) Children / age guidance

justinTime is not directed to children under 13. If you are between 16–17, you may use the app. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13.

9) Security

We take reasonable steps to protect your information. However, no method of storage is 100% secure. Because data is primarily stored on-device, protecting your device (PIN/biometrics) helps protect your data.

10) Changes to this policy

We may update this policy from time to time. We will update the “Effective date” above and, where appropriate, provide notice in the app.

11) Contact

If you have questions or requests about privacy, contact us at: kejolcompany@gmail.com
